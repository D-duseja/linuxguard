What LinuxGuard does

  
LinuxGuard reads Linux permission listings, flags risky patterns, and explains each finding in simple language with a suggested chmod fix. It checks for SUID/SGID files, world-writable files and directories (with sticky-bit awareness), group-writable files, and exposed secrets such as private keys and .env files. Strict mode tightens the rules. Everything runs locally on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
